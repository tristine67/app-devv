# My Favorite Movie: *The Book of Life*

It tells a story about a girl, daughter of the chief in the village, and two young men who are in love with her from childhood. The girl is named **Maria** and she was sent away early in her childhood to learn to be a proper woman. The two young men stay behind and never forget about her. One boy is named **Joaquin** and the other **Manolo**.

---

## The Bet

Two entities made a bet about which boy would eventually win over and marry **Maria**. The entities are namely:
* **Xibalba** *(ruler of the land of the forgotten)*
* **La Muerte** *(ruler of the remembered)*

## The Two Young Men

* Xibalba cheats and gives **Joaquin** a *badge of immortality* giving him an advantage. Joaquin wins Maria's father's heart as he became the hero of the village, ending up in the chief wanting Maria to marry him.
* **Manolo**, on the other hand, loves **guitar** and plays from the heart. However, he comes from a line of bullfighters, and they take pride in their lineage. Manolo had no choice but to follow their footsteps regardless of how much he hated it.
